Trust and safety

Built to be honest about what it knows.

AI explains, a model computes

Every number comes from a documented exposure model. The AI is told never to invent measurements.

Private by design

API keys stay on the server, and household profiles stay on the user's own device.

Clear about its limits

Guidance, not diagnosis. Warning signs always point people to medical care.

Questions, answered.

Where does the air-quality data come from?

Live and forecast data comes from Open-Meteo's air-quality service, which is based on Copernicus CAMS atmospheric models.

Is the AI giving medical advice?

No. Saans offers practical guidance and always directs people to medical care for warning signs such as chest pain or severe breathlessness.

How are the impact numbers calculated?

A transparent exposure model weights the hours you spend indoors, outdoors and commuting. Its assumptions are shown inside the app.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Plans and chat replies can be generated in English or Hindi.
